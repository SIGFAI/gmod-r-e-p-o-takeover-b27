# R.E.P.O. Takeover

**Garry's Mod** × R.E.P.O. · visual · idea by the team

Bring R.E.P.O. into the game in the most visible and funny way. The change shows in the first 30 seconds of play.

> I put R.E.P.O. in Garry's Mod: gold loot with price tags, robot bots hauling it, gnome swarms, and slamming a golden toilet drops its value.

## Install

Unzip the mod folder into a new folder inside garrysmod/addons, start Garry's Mod in Sandbox on any map, and the loot, robots, gnomes and the Huntsman appear on their own. Grab loot with E or the physics gun and carry it into the green zone.
